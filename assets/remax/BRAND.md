# RE/MAX brand notes for the Year in Review posters

Source: Figma "REMAX - Handbook" (file 9KqKqrCRjhWks752uauKb9), Brand & assets page, which quotes the
RE/MAX Brand Identity Manual, 22nd edition (2026). Internal use only.

## Name
- Write **REMAX** in text: all capitals, no slash. RE/MAX only in "RE/MAX, LLC".
- Real agents in the group: **Real REMAX Group** (Real and Group in initial caps).

## Colours (BIM p. 23)
| Name | Hex | Use here |
|---|---|---|
| Primary Red | #FF1200 | accent (replaces Real lime) |
| Primary Blue | #0043FF | main field (replaces Real cobalt) |
| Cream | #F7F5EE | light field, cream logo |
| Black | #000000 | type, black logo |
| Dark Blue | #000E35 | deep field (replaces Real navy) |
| Dark Red | #660000 | red-page extrusion |
| Bridge Red #AA1120, Bridge Blue #0C2749, Sky Blue #A3D4F2, Dark Charcoal #232323, White #FFFFFF | supporting |

- Gradients only within Primary Red or within Primary Blue. No tints.
- Two-Color Angled designs at -27.5 degrees: Primary Blue/Dark Blue, Primary Red/Dark Red, Cream/Black.
- Slash Pattern: lines at -27.5 degrees in one solid Brand Colour.

## Type (BIM p. 21)
Gotham (print), Montserrat for web (Keystone). We use Montserrat.

## Logos
- Primary logotype = wordmark + Balloon, **Black or Cream only** (lockup-black.png, lockup-cream.png).
- Secondary = wordmark alone, any Brand Colour (wordmark.svg).
- Balloon always in colour, never recoloured, tilted, or used as a bullet or pin (balloon.svg).
- Clear space: half the X height on all sides. Never in a box, no drop shadow, no outline.
- Real REMAX agent logo "PART OF REAL // REMAX": REMAX branding must be larger and more prominent.

## AI rule (BIM pp. 5, 105)
Do not put REMAX logos or materials into generative AI tools. RE/MAX versions of our art are recoloured
from our own illustrations in code; logos are placed by hand.
